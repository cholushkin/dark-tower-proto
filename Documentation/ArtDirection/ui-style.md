## UI DESIGN

The UI should follow the same visual language as the characters and world: **geometric, minimal, playful, expressive, and highly readable**. It should feel like a natural part of a whimsical fantasy world, combining clean graphic design with charming medieval-fantasy aesthetics and subtle visual humor.

Use large panels, simple geometric divisions, bold icons, chunky buttons, rounded or slightly angular shapes, and generous negative space. Favor clear structure, strong visual grouping, and playful asymmetry. Avoid dense dashboards, excessive HUD elements, tiny indicators, elaborate ornamentation, heavy textures, and decorative clutter.

Use a warm, vibrant fantasy palette:

- **Deep plum / muted purple** for panel backgrounds, framing, and structural elements.
- **Warm cream / soft beige** for primary information, text, and neutral surfaces.
- **Golden yellow / amber** for important actions, rewards, currency, and highlights.
- **Coral red / pink** for warnings, emphasis, playful accents, and special states.
- **Warm orange / earthy brown** for secondary elements, borders, and supporting details.

Use contrasting colors consistently to communicate interaction states. Active, selected, disabled, and warning states must remain immediately distinguishable.

UI elements should feel **solid, tactile, and slightly chunky**, like simplified fantasy objects made from painted wood, carved stone, or enchanted materials. Use mostly opaque surfaces, flat colors, subtle planar shading, and minimal gradients. Avoid excessive glow, transparency, realistic material textures, and elaborate dimensional effects.

Icons should be **bold, colorful, simplified fantasy illustrations** with recognizable silhouettes and minimal internal detail, matching the visual style of in-game items. Favor expressive shapes and intentional exaggeration over realistic proportions or intricate decoration.

Typography should be playful but highly legible, with a clear hierarchy between headings, primary information, and secondary details. Favor distinctive fantasy-inspired headings combined with simple, readable body text. Avoid overly ornate medieval lettering.

Subtle humor can appear through exaggerated button proportions, amusing icons, playful animations, and charming visual feedback. Interactions should feel satisfying and full of personality without compromising usability or consistency.

Favor **low visual noise, strong contrast, clear hierarchy, and intuitive interaction**. Every UI element must serve clarity, feedback, personality, or gameplay readability.

**The UI must remain readable at small sizes, from a distance, and during active gameplay.** Visual personality should strengthen usability rather than compete with it.
