# ART DIRECTION — STYLIZED COMEDY FANTASY

## OVERALL STYLE

Stylized low-poly fantasy concept art with strong graphic readability, playful exaggeration, and a subtle handcrafted feel. Use large geometric shapes, simplified planes, bold silhouettes, intentional asymmetry, and subtle value changes. Favor clean, angular forms mixed with soft, rounded shapes. Avoid excessive triangulation and overly smooth geometry.

Prioritize visual hierarchy:

**1. Silhouette → 2. Color blocking → 3. Major forms → 4. Minimal details.**

Use mostly flat colors with subtle planar shading and minimal gradients. Define forms through color blocks, silhouettes, and value contrast. **No outlines or line art.** Avoid photorealism, polished CG, sterile vector art, and excessive detail.

Favor simple, chunky, slightly exaggerated proportions with recognizable shapes and charming imperfections. Avoid micro-decoration, excessive textures, random geometric fragmentation, and visual clutter.

## COLOR PALETTE

Use a **warm, vibrant, playful fantasy palette** centered on:

- **Golden yellow, amber, and warm orange** for treasures, highlights, and magical objects.
- **Deep plum, muted purple, and burgundy** for shadows, stone, metal, and structural forms.
- **Warm cream, peach, and soft beige** for organic materials, characters, and neutral surfaces.
- **Bright coral red and pink** for playful accents, magical details, and focal points.
- **Muted earthy browns** for wood, leather, and natural materials.

Favor rich, harmonious color blocking with warm highlights and purple-tinted shadows. Use selective saturation, clear value separation, and minimal gradients. Keep colors expressive without becoming visually noisy.

## FANTASY WORLD & TONE

A whimsical fantasy world built around **parody, absurdity, and playful interpretations of classic fantasy tropes**. The setting is full of incompetent villains, ridiculous monsters, questionable magic, exaggerated treasures, and charmingly dysfunctional fantasy kingdoms.

The world should feel **funny, mischievous, colorful, and slightly chaotic**, with humor expressed primarily through visual design rather than excessive decoration.

Characters and creatures feature expressive silhouettes, amusing proportions, exaggerated features, and instantly recognizable personalities. Even traditionally intimidating monsters, weapons, and magical artifacts should carry a touch of irony or absurdity.

Architecture and environments combine chunky medieval structures, crooked towers, oversized mushrooms, twisted trees, enchanted ruins, and whimsical magical elements. Favor playful asymmetry, exaggerated scale, and memorable shapes.

Magic appears through simple, colorful effects, glowing symbols, enchanted objects, and unexpected visual transformations. Keep magical effects selective and visually readable.

**Humor should emerge naturally from shapes, proportions, expressions, and unexpected combinations.** Avoid overly childish aesthetics, excessive cuteness, and visual slapstick that undermines the world's coherence.

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Every visual element must contribute to silhouette, identity, composition, function, humor, or gameplay readability.

**Artwork must remain recognizable and visually appealing at small sizes.**

Never sacrifice silhouette or readability for detail. Complexity should only be added when it strengthens visual identity, personality, or function.

The overall aesthetic should feel **charming, quirky, irreverent, and full of personality** — a cohesive fantasy world that takes its own ridiculousness seriously enough to make it funny.
